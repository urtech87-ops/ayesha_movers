About AYESHA Movers & Packers

One team for labour, trucks and carpenters. We move homes and offices from door to door, across Bahrain and abroad, day or night.

Who runs it

How we work

Labour included in the price

Packing, carrying, loading and unloading are done by our crew and covered by the price we quote you.

Door to door

We start at your door and finish at the door of your new home or office.

One team, start to finish

Labour, trucks and carpenters all come from us, so you don't have to find a separate truck or carpenter.

Day or night

We work 24 hours, so your move can happen at the time that suits you, including at night.
