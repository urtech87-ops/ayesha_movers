AYESHA Movers & Packers: three logo options

Each option is drawn from carton boxes, in navy, gold and white, with the name on two lines: "AYESHA Movers" over "& Packers". The frames below are the real Home page with only the logo swapped in (header at 375px and 1280px, then the footer). The site itself has not changed.

All text is converted to outlines from the site's own font (Roboto), so the files need no font. Sizes: 44px tall on phones (it can shrink to about 40px on 320px phones so the header stays on one row), 56px on computers and in the footer.

Option A: one carton, tape over the lid

One carton seen from a three-quarter angle, the gold tape running along the lid seam and down the front. "AYESHA" bold, "Movers" medium, "& Packers" regular, all navy (white in the footer).

Option B: three stacked cartons

Two navy cartons with gold tape, a gold carton on top. "& Packers" is in gold: on navy it is the site's gold; on white it is a darker gold (#8A6500, 5.3:1), because the site's gold on white is only 1.8:1 and fails AA for text.

Option C: the open carton that is also a home

My own idea: an open carton whose two lid flaps lean together into a roof, so the box you pack is also the home you move, with "& Packers" printed on a strip of gold packing tape (navy on gold, 9.9:1).
